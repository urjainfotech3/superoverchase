Super Over Chase · Android game

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Super Over Chase is a cricket game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: we do not ask for your name, email or any account, and your game progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some device information to show and measure those ads.

Information stored on your phone

Your coins, stars, unlocked levels, bats, settings and daily streak are saved only inside the app on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner, full-screen and optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and protect these ads from fraud, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address, which gives an approximate location
  • Device and app information, such as phone model, Android version and how ads are interacted with
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data

This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses data from apps that show its ads at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice later. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play only tells the game whether you own the purchase, so it can switch ads off, including after you reinstall.

Children

Super Over Chase is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and check purchases. The game itself plays offline.
  • Vibration: for hit and wicket effects. You can turn it off in the game.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information. Data collected by AdMob is handled by Google as described above.

This website

This website (urjainfotech3.github.io) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits and see which links bring visitors. It does not use cookies, does not store anything on your device and does not identify individual visitors. The Super Over Chase app itself does not use it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
